**Mission:** income streams that Claude agents build once and then *operate* day-to-day, with your 2–5 hrs/week reserved for what only a human can do (accounts/KYC, sales, posting under your identity).

**Method:** 28-agent research fleet run 2026-07-14 — 8 sector scouts (47 raw ideas) → 10 deep-dive validators → 10 adversarial skeptics whose only job was to kill the numbers. ~2.4M tokens of research, ~670 web searches/fetches, every figure sourced from live 2025–26 data. Currency: ₹88 ≈ $1.

## 0. The honest headline (read this first)

**No idea survived skeptic scrutiny *as a hands-off money printer*.** All 10 validated ideas got "worth-testing" from analysts and "does not survive" from skeptics — not because the products are bad, but because of four structural facts of 2026:

1. **The passive playbooks are dead.** Google's AI Overviews cut organic CTR 34–61% and its scaled-content-abuse policy targets programmatic sites; YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy demonetizes AI content farms (Jan 2026 purge: ~$9.8M/yr of channels stripped); Google froze job-board Indexing API access (Oct 2025). Every classic "build it, SEO does the rest" route is structurally impaired.
2. **AI made building free — for everyone.** Every niche the fleet examined already has 5–25 AI-built clones (8+ cron-monitor clones, 10+ GMP trackers, 25+ GEO trackers, 8+ competitor-digest tools). If Claude can build it in 2 weeks, so can everyone else. **The build is no longer the business.**
3. **The base rates are brutal.** 54% of Stripe-verified indie products earn exactly $0. ~70% of micro-SaaS never pass $1K MRR. Median Chrome extension: 18 users. Median new Shopify app: ~$0. Median newsletter at 1,000 subs: $100–500/mo. Any pitch quoting a winner (Healthchecks $14K MRR, Superpower $20K/mo) is quoting a survivor, usually with a 5–9 year head start.
4. **"Autonomy" conflates two different things.** Keeping software running: 85–90% automatable (true, verified, and Claude genuinely does this well on your existing launchd/Telegram/tunnel infra). Making strangers trust and pay you: ~0% automatable. **Revenue tracks founder-identity distribution hours, not agent hours.**

**What this actually means for you:** the realistic model is not "Claude earns while I sleep." It is:

> **Claude compresses build cost to ~zero and runs 85–90% of operations. That makes experiments so cheap you can run demand tests *before building*, kill fast, and stack the 1–2 survivors. Your 2–5 hrs/week of sales/marketing — which you said you're willing to do — is the input that revenue is actually proportional to.**

That is a real, defensible edge. It is just a different business than the one the internet sells.

## 2. Tier 1 — Build-worthy, in order (with the demand gate that must pass FIRST)

The fleet's most useful output isn't the ideas — it's the **pre-build demand gates**. Each costs days, not months. Claude preps everything; you run the human touchpoints. If a gate fails, you've spent ~₹0 and killed a bad bet. If it passes, you build with evidence.

### 2.1 Podcast clipping desk for B2B SaaS podcasts — the highest honest P50 on the board

**What it is:** Done-for-you repurposing at $500–900/mo per show: every episode auto-becomes 10 branded 9:16 captioned clips + LinkedIn posts + show notes + newsletter draft. Sits in the empty middle between $29/mo DIY tools (OpusClip) and $2,000+/mo agencies (ThePod.fm charges $1.5–2.5k for less).

**Why it ranks #1 for you specifically:** its weakness — revenue requires selling — is the one input you explicitly said you'll supply. It has the fastest time-to-first-revenue (4–6 wks), near-zero COGS (WhisperX local + ffmpeg on your Mac ≈ 95% margin), and real comparables (Fame: $20K MRR, team of 3; Counterweight: $10K+/mo solo-with-contractors).

**Skeptic's honest verdict:** "a sales job wearing an automation costume." Adjusted P50 $300/mo (≈ coin-flip on holding one client through churn); P90 $3.5–5.5k needs a permanent 4–6 hrs/wk sales grind. Churn is brutal (podfade + 3–6 month client churn) so outreach never stops.

**⚡ DEMAND GATE (before building anything):** Claude scrapes ~200 active B2B SaaS podcasts (Listen Notes/Apple), builds 20 unsolicited sample kits **using off-the-shelf tools** (no pipeline built yet), drafts personalized outreach; you send under your identity over 3–4 weeks. **Kill if no discovery call by week 4 or no paying pilot (₹20k first-month) by week 6.** Only a converted pilot justifies building the real pipeline.

- **Claude runs (~85–90%):** RSS watch → transcribe → moment-score → render clips → draft copy → your Telegram approval queue → schedule via Ayrshare; weekly per-client reports; prospect-list refresh + outreach drafts; invoicing drafts.
- **You do:** send outreach, 20-min close calls (US/EU evening IST), taste-check each client's first 2 episodes, monthly check-ins.
- **Money rail:** Razorpay International (MoneySaver export account, ~1% fee, auto e-FIRC) or PayPal/Wise. (Paddle/Lemon Squeezy don't cover human-delivered services.)

### 2.2 GEO tracker — AI-search share-of-voice at the $19–49 gap

**What it is:** "PromptPulse" — samples ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Google-AIO daily via **official APIs** for a customer's 10–25 buying prompts, reports brand share-of-voice vs competitors with confidence intervals. Telegram-first, honest-methodology positioning (the category's known weakness is accuracy hype — your preregistered-eval discipline is literally the differentiator).

**Why it's real:** the budget line exists and is exploding — Peec AI hit $10M ARR in 16 months; bootstrapped LLM Pulse hit mid-five-figure MRR in a year; a genuine price gap exists below $59 (Otterly $29 floor, Trakkr repriced to $100, Knowatoa $59+). The architecture is your NSE signal engine with different data sources — nightly sampler, scorer, DB, Telegram alerter, Next.js dashboard.

**Skeptic's honest verdict:** adjusted P50 $150/mo. Every winner in this category grew on founder-led distribution (demos, webinars, personal brand); 25+ competitors incl. free tiers (Semrush free checker); COGS on daily sampling is 50–60% if not rationed. As pitched at 2–3 hrs/wk it fails; at 6–10 hrs/wk for two quarters it's a different (viable) business.

**⚡ DEMAND GATE:** 15 conversations with SEO-agency owners / indie SaaS founders (Claude drafts DMs, finds targets; you send). Require 3+ credible "yes at $29–49/mo" — calendar-booked demo or prepaid link, not politeness. No build until then.

- **Money rail:** Dodo Payments (Bengaluru MoR, onboards Indian sole props, ~6% effective, INR settlement + FIRA) or Paddle.
- **Kill criteria:** <4 paying customers or <$150 MRR by week 8 post-launch; COGS >35% after one optimization pass; month-2 churn >25%.

### 2.3 GST compliance suite for Indian Shopify merchants — marketplace does the distribution

**What it is:** Shopify embedded app: GST-compliant invoices/credit notes, e-invoice IRN/QR push, GSTR-1/IMS-ready exports, Tally/Zoho sync. $5–49/mo tiers via **Shopify Billing** — the cleanest rail on the whole board (100% of first $1M lifetime revenue is yours, payouts to Indian bank, zero payment code).

**Why it's on the list:** the only Tier-1 idea where a marketplace does distribution instead of your hours. The niche demonstrably pays (WebPlanex: 9 years, 461 five-star reviews, $5–50/mo tiers); Indian Shopify stores +32% YoY.

**Skeptic's honest verdict:** adjusted P50 $150/mo. Small captured market (~900 total reviews across all GST apps in 9 years), two 5.0-rated incumbents with review-count gravity, e-invoicing wedge legally irrelevant to most (B2C exempt; ₹5cr+ B2B only), review queue adds 4+ weeks, and a tax-math bug is trust-fatal. Real ceiling ≈ $2–3k/mo with exceptional D2C-community distribution.

**⚡ DEMAND GATE:** Claude drafts a teardown of the top-2 incumbents' gaps from their own reviews (feature requests, complaints); you post in 2–3 Indian D2C communities asking "would you switch for X?" and recruit 5 beta merchants. No beta interest → kill before the $19 partner fee.

Zero revenue expectation, zero billing/KYC hours, and it becomes the distribution asset (blog posts, HN cred) for whichever Tier-1 play you run. Claude can build and maintain this ~100% autonomously.

## 6. Portfolio math — what "Claude earning in the background" honestly looks like

Assumes you run the demand gates, build only what passes, and reliably spend 4–6 focused hrs/week on distribution (your stated budget's upper edge). Streams don't stack linearly — your sales hours are the shared bottleneck — so the honest portfolio is **one sales-led service + one marketplace-distributed product + the OSS tool**:

| Milestone | Pessimistic (P10) | **Honest median (P50)** | Good case (P90) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| Month 3 | ₹0 | ₹8–15k/mo (~₹300–500/day) | ₹60–90k/mo |
| Month 6 | ₹0 | ₹20–35k/mo (~₹700–1,200/day) | ₹1.5–2.5L/mo |
| Month 12 | ₹0 | **₹35–55k/mo (~₹1,200–1,800/day)** | ₹3–6L/mo (~₹10–20k/day) |

Two things move you from P50 toward P90: (1) consistency of the weekly sales hours over 12 months — this is the single highest-beta variable; (2) killing losers fast at their pre-registered kill criteria and reallocating the freed agent-capacity — the same discipline as your trading research. What does *not* move the needle: more agent hours, more features, more automation polish.
